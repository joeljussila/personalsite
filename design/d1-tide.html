JussilaCurious about the world from first principles. Engineer by training, former national-level athlete, permanently mid-sidequest.

I believe technology is how we design a world we actually want to live in, and I want to play my own part in that. Most of what I do starts with taking something apart to see what it was really for.

The rest of the time I am somewhere else. A hundred and twenty-one places so far, eighty-three of them photographed, and a standing preference for the ones involving a rope, a board, or a long way down.
