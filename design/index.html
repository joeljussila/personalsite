Five directions

Working prototypes, not pictures. Each one carries the whole site — home, the globe with its real 121 places and 83 photographs, the blog index and a post — so a direction can be judged on how it behaves, not only on how it looks. Every background is generated in code; nothing from the reference images is used.

Drafted 9 September 2026 · Pick one, then it gets built properly

  1. 01 Tide Indigo woodblock. A hatched swell rises from the foot of the window while the words sit on clear paper. Cormorant Garamond set large and light. The globe is engraved — land filled with horizontal hatching, coastlines in ink. Indigo / creamSeaPainterly
  2. 02 Cartograph The page as a survey sheet: ruled border, corner registration marks, a title block along the bottom carrying the metadata. Contour lines breathe underneath. Spectral and Plex Mono. The globe is a wireframe and the places are surveyor's crosses. Prussian / manilaMountainsTechnical
  3. 03 Grain Black, white and grey. Archivo set enormous and tight against a hard twelve-column grid. Behind it a field of grey rectangles crossfading over minutes under heavy film grain. The loudest of the five and the most contemporary. Black / whiteAerialBrutalist
  4. 04 Hachure A rare-book title page on bone paper. EB Garamond centred, an ornament, justified body text with a drop cap. Engraved water fills the margins — ink pooling and thinning across a flow field — and leaves a clear oval where the words are. Black / boneWaterBookish
  5. 05 Nocturne The dark one, for holding against what you have live now. Same restraint as the current site but indigo instead of gold: columns of blurred blue light drifting behind a fine vertical comb, Newsreader on near-black. The globe glows. Indigo / blackAtmosphereContinuity

All five share proto.js, which carries the real orthographic projection, hemisphere clipping and drag behaviour lifted from places/index.html. A direction changes the globe by setting CSS custom properties on the canvas — nothing else. Photographs are duotoned into each palette in the grid and released to full colour when you open one.
